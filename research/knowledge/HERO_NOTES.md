This is mechanical capability under constructed starting conditions, not proof of useful routing or score gains in natural games.

The Vanguard low-score episode reconstruction matched all commands and hashes: eligible camps appeared on15/1553active decisions and were selected every time. Arcanist and Druid also had health, retreat and competing-target restrictions. Those diagnostics identify possible interventions; they do not establish causality.

The relh169 study's 100 late own draft states offered only melee options. A learned draft head cannot select an unavailable carry. Separate legal draft opportunity from whether our controller knows how to extract income from the selected class.
