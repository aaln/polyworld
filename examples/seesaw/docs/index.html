Seesaw

Two children, one board, five minutes. Have fun together without making the other sick. The only language is a face: smile, frown, or a generated expression. The pair score is the same on both seats.

The playground

Lila sits west, Nico east. Pumping adds weight on the way down and lightens on the way up. Every seed hides weather (wind, wet, heat) and two inner lives (tired, nauseous, hungry, wired, …). Riders see coarse weather bands and the partner's lean, pump, and face. They never see the partner's fun, nausea, or preferred intensity.

  • Fun: comfort when the ride sits near your hidden sweet-spot. Both-comfortable ticks pay extra.
  • Breaks: rest() hops off and walks to a bench. Nausea fades faster there. Lean or pump walks you back onto your seat.
  • Score: 2 * min(fun) + sum/4. Making the other child miserable cannot win.

The bots

Each rider is one BASIC program deciding eight ticks at a time: lean, pump, rest, express. The cooperative rider pumps in rhythm, gets off when queasy, and eases off on a frown. The selfish rider ignores the partner. 

nim r examples/seesaw/seesaw.nim --bot examples/seesaw/players/base.bas:2

nim r -d:headless examples/seesaw/seesaw.nim --seed 1988 \
  --bot examples/seesaw/players/base.bas:2 \
  --record examples/seesaw/replays/demo.replay

nim r examples/seesaw/seesaw.nim --replay examples/seesaw/replays/demo.replay
Every game is deterministic: same seed, same bots, same five minutes — recorded to a replay that verifies bit-exactly on playback.
