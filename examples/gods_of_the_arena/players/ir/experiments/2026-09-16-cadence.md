## Mechanism and caveats

The unchanged published engine lands basic hits at 45% of swing duration and
resets the swing when marching. A verified Crossbow replay checkpoint followed
by legal one-tick post-hit movement produced repeated 17-tick hit intervals,
versus 36 for attack-only. It produced seven hits versus three in the attack-only
probe, but every arm died. This is a ten-second open-loop mechanics probe, with
recorded peer commands and target schedule, not competitive evidence. Its first
version confounded target retention; that version is preserved alongside the
corrected instrument and results.

Expected benefit: brief recovery steps increase damage opportunities without
cancelling a windup. Expected failure: added action switching or lost movement
raises deaths, reduces spell output, or fails to improve fort wins. One-tick
movement often only turns; do not describe it as effective kiting. Emergency
retreats are a separate longer behavior with separation feedback. Earlier
long-retreat and pressure failures are retained; this is a new interaction.
