The hypothesis and the isolated change

Permanent gear can fill all six inventory slots and prevent emergency healing purchases. The IR candidate caps equipment at four, counting only successful purchases within each decision. Targeting, sustain rules, consumption, equipment order and fallback match Duelist.

The tradeoff is fewer permanent stats. Two consumable slots can still be occupied by competing healing or mana items; reserved capacity does not guarantee every healing purchase succeeds.

The gate stayed fixed

Discovery required at least four added wins and observed equipment pressure in three classes. The candidate gained three wins, so the 120 planned independent confirmation seeds remain unobserved. Had it qualified, confirmation required gains of at least 5 points over Duelist and 10 points over default baseline, paired exact p < .025 for each, and the predeclared regression checks.

The six discovery seeds do not establish statistical superiority. Local defaults also cannot establish strength against the hosted field. No threshold was changed after looking at results.
